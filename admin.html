How to Sync Changes Across ALL Devices & Hosting (Netlify/Vercel)

Follow these 3 easy steps to deploy your Control Panel edits so that visitors on ANY mobile or computer automatically see your new skills, bio, photos & projects:

  1. Edit & Save: Make your edits in the Control Panel and click Save All.
  2. Download Master Data JSON: Click the button below to download your master portfolio-data.json file.
  3. Replace in Project Folder: Save the downloaded file into assets/data/portfolio-data.json in your project directory before deploying to Netlify/Vercel/GitHub. Every device worldwide will load your new content automatically!
